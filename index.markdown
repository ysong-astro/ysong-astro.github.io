  <p>I am an observational astronomer at the <a href="https://www.mpifr-bonn.mpg.de/2169/en" target="_blank">Max-Planck-Institut für Radioastronomie</a> in Bonn, Germany, where I work as a postdoc researcher in the <a href="https://www.mpifr-bonn.mpg.de/research/galaxies" target="_blank">Star Formation and Galaxy Evolution group</a> led by Prof. Dr. Amélie Saintonge. Previously I was a postdoctoral fellow at the <a href="https://www.eso.org/public/" target="_blank">European Southern Observatory</a> and <a href="https://www.almaobservatory.org/en/home/" target="_blank">Joint ALMA Observatory</a> in Chile, where I supported ALMA operation as an Astronomer on Duty and VLBI-friend. I obtained my PhD in Astronomy in 2022 at <a href="https://astronomy.as.virginia.edu/" target="_blank">the University of Virginia</a> and <a href="https://public.nrao.edu/" target="_blank">the National Radio Astronomy Observatory</a>, supervised by Prof. Aaron Evans. 
  </p>
  <p> My current role focuses on exploring the technical challenges and opportunities for <a href="https://www.atlast.uio.no/" target="_blank">the Atacama Large Aperture Submillimeter Telescope </a> via hands-on experimentation with datasets from <a href="https://www.apex-telescope.org/ns/" target="_blank">APEX</a>, <a href="https://iram-institute.org/observatories/30-meter-telescope/" target="_blank">IRAM30m</a>, <a href="https://greenbankobservatory.org/" target="_blank">GBT</a> and <a href="https://almascience.eso.org/" target="_blank">ALMA</a> of the Milky Way and nearby galaxies. My main scientific interests lie in <a href="/science" target="_blank">the fascinating chaos of local merger-driven starbursts</a>, and I utilize a wide range of facilities including <a href="https://public.nrao.edu/telescopes/vla/" target="_blank">VLA</a> , <a href="https://almascience.eso.org/" target="_blank">ALMA</a> and <a href="https://esawebb.org/" target="_blank">JWST</a> to characterize their spatially-resolved properties and study the underlying physics of star formation, supermassive blackhole accretion and feedback associated with these extreme, dense, dusty environments. </p>

  <p> In my free time I enjoy all formats of science fiction, and spending time in nature. </p>
