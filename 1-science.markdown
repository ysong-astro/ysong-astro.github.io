<div style="max-width: 800px; margin: 0 auto; line-height: 1.5; text-align: justify;">
I am fascinated by all things related to galaxy interactions and mergers. My own research has been focused on uncovering the dust-obscured stellar and black-hole growth triggered by these processes, mainly utilizing high-resolution radio/sub-mm observations. I am scientifically involved in the following collaborations: 
</div>
<br>
> <a href="https://goals.ipac.caltech.edu/" target="_blank">Great Observatories All-sky LIRG Survey (GOALS)</a>
<div style="max-width: 800px; margin: 0 auto; line-height: 1.5; text-align: justify;">
  <img src="{{ '/assets/vla.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Outflow of IRASF01364-1042" style="float: left; width: 220px; height: 300px; border:4px solid #ddd;; border-radius: 5px; margin-right: 40px; margin-bottom: 9px; object-fit: cover;">
  

  <p> GOALS is a multi-wavelength collaboration dedicated to study the most extreme star-forming galaxies in the local Universe, which are often triggered by galaxy interaction and mergers. Powerful nuclear starbursts fuelled by gas tidally driven into the galaxy centers produce copious amount of dust that bias classical UV/optical tracers. Using multi-frequency multi-configuration VLA observations of 70 GOALS systems, I characterize the intrinsic star formation rates and energy density of compact nuclei ubiquitously found in these systems and uncover evidence of heavily-obscured AGN. </p>

  <div style="clear: both;"></div>
</div>

<br>
> <a href="https://vivian-u.github.io/mauna/mauna.html" target="_blank"> Multiphase Analysis of (U)LIRG Nuclear Activity (MAUNA)</a>
<div style="max-width: 800px; margin: 0 auto; line-height: 1.5; text-align: justify;">
  <img src="{{ '/assets/outflow.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Outflow of IRASF01364-1042" style="float: right; width: 220px; height: 330px; border:4px solid #ddd;; border-radius: 5px; margin-left: 40px; margin-bottom: 9px; object-fit: cover;">
  

  <p> MAUNA aims to link the extreme nuclear activities found in local interacting and merging galaxies to the large scale multi-phase galactic feedback observed in these systems, using both optical and near-/mid-IR IFU observations from Keck, VLT and JWST. I contribute my radio/sub-mm expertise to this effort via providing further context on the plasma/dust continuum emission arising from the nuclei as well as looking for feedback in the cold molecular gas phase which generally dominates the outflow masses. </p>

  <div style="clear: both;"></div>
</div>

<br>
> <a href="https://www.alma-mingle.com" target="_blank"> Merger-INduced starbursts and the Gas Lifecycle Examination (MINGLE) </a>
<div style="max-width: 800px; margin: 0 auto; line-height: 1.5; text-align: justify;">

  <p> MINGLE is an ALMA Cycle 12 Large Program with 111hrs of 12m time dedicated to reveal the molecular gas cloud properties in local 22 interacting galaxy pairs at 70pc resolution, mainly traced by CO (J = 2-1) emission. I am part of the data reduction team and co-PI team with a special focus on compact dust continuum sources and CO isotopologue lines simultaneously uncovered in these exciting new observations.  </p>

  <div style="clear: both;"></div>
</div>

> <a href="https://www.alma-mingle.com" target="_blank"> Dense Extragalactic GBT+Argus Survey (DEGAS)</a>
<div style="max-width: 800px; margin: 0 auto; line-height: 1.5; text-align: justify;">

  <p> DEGAS is a large program on GBT/Argus dedicated to study kpc-scale properties of dense gas tracers, i.e., 13CO, C18O, HCN, HCO+, in the centers of 36 nearby galaxies, complementing existing dense gas surveys with IRAM30m and ALMA covering different spatial scales. </p>
